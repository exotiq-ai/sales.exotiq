Cheat sheet

Keep this in your head.

  • Lead with money. Revenue first, time second, risk third.
  • Anchor every price to revenue. Never quote a per-car number alone.
  • Say "up to 30%" as illustrative. It varies by market and fleet, and you validate with their data.
  • Name Rari every time. The voice agent is the thing no one else has.
  • A precision instrument, not a hood ornament. Confident, benefit-first, short sentences. No jargon, no "streamline" or "leverage."
  • No em-dashes when you write your own outreach. Periods or commas.
  • Spelling vs sound. Write "exotiq," say "Exotique."
  • Talk to operators like the sharp business people they are. Never talk down.
